How to check any of this yourself

This site is generated by an open pipeline. Every figure is extracted from the documents listed under Sources, and each source file's SHA-256 hash is published so you can confirm your copy is the same file ours came from. Rebuild the dataset with make etl and the output is byte-for-byte identical — so you do not have to trust us, including about this.

An honest limitation. The town's annual financial reports for FY2018 through FY2025 are scanned images. The text buried in them comes from character recognition, and it transposes digits — a page that plainly reads 4,610,003 comes back as 460,100,3, and a stated 61.02% comes back as 601.2%. No figure on this page is taken from those text layers. That is also why the audited decade of history is not charted here yet: it must be transcribed from the page images first, and doing it carelessly would publish wrong numbers about real people.
