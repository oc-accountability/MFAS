How to check this yourself

This site is generated from a public data pipeline. The figures are extracted from the documents listed in Source documents below, and each source file's SHA-256 hash is recorded so you can confirm you are looking at the same file we were.

An honest limitation. The town's annual financial reports for FY2018 through FY2024 are scanned images. Their embedded text is produced by character recognition and it transposes digits — it renders 4,610,003 as 460,100,3. No figure on this page is taken from those text layers. That is why the audited multi-year history is not charted here yet: it has to be transcribed from the page images first, and doing that carelessly would publish wrong numbers about real people.
