Your town, your money

Hillsborough publishes its budget in documents that run to hundreds of pages. This page turns them into the answer you actually want: what the town costs your household, what that money pays for, and whether the town's finances are in good shape.

Nothing here is our opinion. Every figure comes from the town's own published documents, and each one shows you the document and page it came from so you can check it.

Who made this, and why you can check it

This page was built for the Orange County Efficiency & Accountability Initiative by residents, not by the town. That is exactly why it shows its sources for everything: you should not have to take our word for a single number. The whole dataset is rebuilt from the town's documents by an open pipeline, and rebuilding it reproduces this page byte for byte.

What is still missing, and why. The town's audited annual reports for 2018 through 2024 are scanned images, and the text hidden inside them scrambles digits — a page that plainly reads 4,610,003 comes back as 460,100,3. Nothing on this page is taken from that text.

FY2025 is different: it was also published as a proper digital file, so the audited figures above are read directly from it with no character recognition involved. For the earlier years we have now tested whether fresh recognition can read these statements reliably — on the one report that exists in both forms it reproduced 141 of 141 figures exactly. So the earlier decade is no longer out of reach; it is simply work not yet done, and it will only be published once each page's own arithmetic has been checked. We would rather show you less than show you something wrong.
